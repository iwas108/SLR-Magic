Calibration Pool Assignment Cookbook

To prevent down-funnel data starvation—where a standard random sample of 100 papers yields too few surviving candidates to calibrate and train the deeper analytical stages—the calibration corpus is designed as a Decoupled Parallel Injection Pool. Rather than using sequential stages or purely random samples, reviewers execute targeted manual screening to select 100 mathematically independent, non-overlapping papers.

These papers are pre-allocated into three distinct sub-populations, engineered specifically to stress-test and evaluate the respective AI agents independently, none of these papers should overlap with other Pool. :

Pool A: Metadata Filtering Calibration (Target: 50 papers)

Selected to calibrate and stress-test Stage 1 (The Fast Filter). The human panel manually identifies papers that meet baseline metadata criteria, combined with targeted semantic boundary traps (e.g., abstracts containing keywords but describing out-of-scope concepts, or boundary topics that test filter precision). Choose 50% good example, and 50% bad example.

Pool B: Full-Text Structural Integration (Target: 30 papers)

Selected to calibrate Stage 2.1 (The Gatekeeper). Reviewers bypass Stage 1 to hand-pick papers that test full-text relevance: half demonstrating clear inclusion, and the other half representing complex exclusions (e.g., studies that discuss theoretical models without any empirical implementation). Choose 50% good example, and 50% bad example.

Pool C: Methodological Appraisal & Extraction (Target: 20 papers)

Selected to stress-test Stage 2.2 (The Scientist) and Stage 2.3 (The Miner). Reviewers hand-pick high-maturity, cross-domain papers known to pass Stages 1 and 2.1. These papers must display a deliberate variance in methodological quality (spanning weak methodologies to highly rigorous ones) and contain dense, structured extraction parameters. Choose 50% good example, and 50% bad example.
