Welcome to SLR-Magic! ✨

Your AI-powered assistant for Systematic Literature Reviews.

SLR-Magic automates the tedious parts of research, from screening thousands of abstracts to extracting data from full-text PDFs.

How It Works

1. Abstract Screening: AI reviews titles and abstracts to filter irrelevant papers based on your criteria.

2. Full-Text Screening: Upload PDFs to Drive. The multi-stage AI (Gatekeeper, Scientist, Miner) reads the full text to confirm inclusion and extract data.

Quality Check & Data

Quality Check: The system samples AI decisions for human review to ensure high accuracy (Agreement Rate, Recall).

Data Collection: Automatically parse structured data from the "The Miner" stage into clean columns.
